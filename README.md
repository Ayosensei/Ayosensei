### 🚀 Top 5 Featured Projects

#### 1. [Yarn (VoiceLog)](https://yarn-voicelog.vercel.app) — AI Voice-to-Text Logbook Assistant
> *An offline-first voice-to-text logbook PWA purpose-built for Nigerian university and polytechnic students on SWEP, SIWES, and IT attachments.*

* **The Problem:** Physical logbooks have rigid line constraints (2–6 lines/day). Generic AI tools generate bloated 5-paragraph essays in passive voice that coordinators reject.
* **The Solution:** Students speak naturally in English or Nigerian workshop lingo for 30s. Yarn transforms it into concise, active first-person ("I" / "We") bullet points strictly calibrated to their paper box limits.
* **Key Features:** Google Gemini AI prompt calibration, offline-first audio caching, zero-friction Guest mode with automatic Google OAuth data migration, and one-click export to Word (.docx) & PDF with official ITF supervisor signing tables.
* **Stack:** `Next.js 16` · `React 19` · `FastAPI` · `Python 3.10` · `Google Gemini AI` · `Supabase (PostgreSQL + Auth)` · `PWA`
* **Links:** [Live Web App](https://yarn-voicelog.vercel.app) • [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/Ayosensei/yarn-voicelog)

#### 2. [Looku Looku 👀](https://github.com/Ayosensei/looku-looku) — Automated B2B Lead Discovery & Audit Engine
> *A high-impact lead generation tool that locates offline businesses, audits digital presence, and enriches verified contact handles for client acquisition.*

* **The Architecture:** 3-stage automated pipeline:
  1. **Discovery:** Scrapes local businesses geographically using OpenStreetMap / Overpass API with zero external API costs.
  2. **Qualification:** Automated website presence and health scoring algorithm (0–100 scale).
  3. **Enrichment:** Compiles direct phone numbers, email addresses, and social handles into an actionable outreach pipeline.
* **Stack:** `React` · `TypeScript` · `FastAPI (Python)` · `SQLite` · `OpenStreetMap / Overpass API` · `Tailwind CSS`
* **Links:** [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/Ayosensei/looku-looku)
